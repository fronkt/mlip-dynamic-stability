Thank you for handling the manuscript again. I enclose the revised manuscript, a copy with the

The referee's four comments asked for the physics behind four results. The main changes are:

- for comment 1, a pre-registered multi-mode SSCHA test on one unit (BaTiO₃ with MACE-MP-0 at
  100 K). It did not support the explanation the first revision gave for the SSCHA's stable calls
  on the ferroelectric perovskites: the free energy at the screen's displaced minimum lies above the
  cubic one, so the two methods disagree through mode coupling, and Section 3.3 now says so,
  together with what this implies for the screen's own recall;
- for comment 2, a quantitative account of why sub-percent lattice changes decide these calls, a
  five-model lattice sweep, and an explicit statement of the constant-volume protocol;
- for comment 3, the SSCHA convergence failure rewritten as a lesson in validating a stochastic
  minimiser, with a population-by-population table;
- for comment 4, the "guardrail" framing removed and an analysis of why force-level ensemble
  spread misses errors the five models share.

number in the manuscript and ESI is checked against them by the deposited scripts.
