### 1.5 bcc 2×2×2 v 3×3×3 (C5), what it is

The converged Zr/MatterSim pair at 300 K (+0.92 in 2×2×2, −0.92 in 3×3×3, both converged and
start-independent) is a change of **q-set**, not a convergence test: N is commensurate only with
2×2×2, ω (q = ⅔⟨111⟩) only with 3×3×3. The 3×3×3 instability is therefore most plausibly the ω
mode entering the cell. The grid's whole bcc block is 3×3×3, so every bcc change against
production (9 converged S→U units, all MatterSim or ORB-v2) mixes cell and convergence. No nested
4×4×4 (N) / 6×6×6 (ω) run exists.
