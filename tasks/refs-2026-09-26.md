papers behind R1.3, judged on their merits, and (3) published first-principles numbers for cross-checking C3a.
The manuscript has not been edited.

**How each entry was checked**
- Metadata comes from the Crossref record (`api.crossref.org/works/<doi>`): title, full author list, journal,
  volume, pages/article number, and year. OpenAlex supplied work type and abstract. Every DOI below resolved,
  and its title and authors matched.
- A quote marked **[abstract]** was taken from the Crossref or OpenAlex abstract of the published version.
- A quote marked **[full text: source]** was pulled by a script from the full text named, usually an arXiv PDF
  and sometimes the publisher PDF or Europe PMC XML. In those quotes, ligatures (ﬁ) and PDF line-break
  hyphenation were normalised and citation superscripts were dropped. Nothing else was changed.
  arXiv quotes carry their version, because published text can differ.
- Adversarial pass: each entry was also checked for a DOI/title mismatch, a quote that is missing from the
  text, and a source saying the opposite. Entries that failed are under REJECTED at the end, with the reason.

Status legend: **VERIFIED** = metadata, quote and claim all checked. **VERIFIED-RESTRICTED** = usable only
for the narrower claim stated. **PREPRINT** = arXiv record verified, not peer-reviewed. **UNVERIFIED** = could
not be confirmed at the source; do not use as is.

### F1 Deng *et al.*, "Systematic softening in universal machine learning interatomic potentials", VERIFIED
- DOI 10.1038/s41524-024-01500-6. Authors: Bowen Deng, Yunyeong Choi, Peichen Zhong, Janosh Riebesell,
  Shashwat Anand, Zhuohan Li, KyuJung Jun, Kristin A. Persson, Gerbrand Ceder. *npj Comput. Mater.* **11**, 9
  (2025). Primary research. The arXiv preprint (2405.07105) had a different title: "Overcoming systematic softening
  … by fine-tuning".
- [abstract] "In this study, we highlight a consistent potential energy surface (PES) softening effect in three
  uMLIPs: M3GNet, CHGNet, and MACE-MP-0, which is characterized by energy and force underprediction in
  atomic-modeling benchmarks including surfaces, defects, solid-solution energetics, ion migration barriers,
  phonon vibration modes, and general high-energy states."
- [full text: publisher PDF] "The distribution of these ratios is shown in Fig. 4b, which demonstrates that the
  majority (>90%) of materials are found to be softened in uMLIPs compared to DFT, with the phonon frequency
  underpredicted." The set is 229 PhononDB materials with a PBEsol reference and NAC removed.
- [full text: publisher PDF] "We demonstrate that this systematic PES softening can be effectively mitigated by
  fine-tuning with a minimal amount of data points."
- [full text: publisher PDF] "We find that a simple linear correction derived from a single DFT reference label is
  sufficient to remove much of the PES softening issue in a specific chemical system of interest, significantly
  enhancing the performance and robustness of uMLIPs for a given application."
- **Supports:** "Universal MLIPs, including CHGNet and MACE-MP-0 from our set, systematically soften the PES.
  They underpredict the maximum phonon frequency in >90% of 229 materials (the metric is the ratio of maximum
  frequencies, uMLIP/DFT). The error is systematic enough that fine-tuning on very few DFT labels removes much of
  it."
- **Restriction (adversarial pass):** the fine-tuning experiments are on **forces** and surface energies (CHGNet,
  1 or 10 labels, WBM structures). The paper does **not** re-run phonon spectra after fine-tuning. Do not write that
  it shows fine-tuning fixes phonon softening; cite F2 or P1 for that.
