## 2. Task 2: E2 interpreted

### 2.1 Convergence (cv_, 90 jobs)

Pre-registered criterion (docstring): for each of k 0.15 A^-1 / ecut x1.3 / both, depth within 5 %
AND the same call at all five T, on all three MACE-MP-0 deciding paths.

| System | Path | Prod depth (meV) | k | ecut | both | Calls changed | Edge |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| BaTiO3 | mace q(1/2,0,1/2) b0 | 73.5 | -0.1 % | +1.7 % | +1.6 % | 0 | no |
| KNbO3 | mace q(0,1/2,1/2) b0 | 87.8 | -0.2 % | -0.2 % | -0.4 % | 0 | no |
| CsSnBr3 | mace q(1/2,1/2,1/2) b0 | 127.2 | **-6.8 %** | -0.0 % | **-6.9 %** | 0 | yes |

Can claim: by the criterion fixed in advance the production settings are NOT converged, and the
failure is in the CsSnBr3 well depth under k-refinement only; every call on all three paths is
unchanged at every temperature in all variants; BaTiO3 and KNbO3 depths move by at most 1.7 %. The
CsSnBr3 depth is a scan-edge value (a lower bound) in every variant, and the denser mesh makes it
shallower (127.2 -> 118.5 meV), i.e. toward "stable", without changing a call.
Cannot claim: convergence for the other four models' paths (only the MACE path per system was
run), or "converged" without qualification. Must report the verdict as the criterion gives it.

### 2.2 Functional (xs_, 230 jobs; PBEsol via input_dft on SSSP PBE pseudos, MLIP geometry)

60 ladder units (BaTiO3, KNbO3, CsSnBr3 x 5 models x 4 T), 23 deciding paths.

Left wrong under both: KNbO3 600 K for CHGNet, MACE-MP-0, MatterSim, ORB-v2; BaTiO3/ORB-v2 300 K;
CsSnBr3/ORB-v2 300 K. The 8 BaTiO3/KNbO3 300 K corrections hold under PBEsol (PBEsol deepens
those wells). Own-model / PBEsol ratio on the 12 BTO/KNO deciding paths of the four models:
0.28-0.68 (median 0.46). CsSnBr3: own/PBE 0.57-2.17, own/PBEsol 1.36-6.85 (MACE, MatterSim,
SevenNet-0: 2.4-3.1).

Can claim: at fixed geometry the CsSnBr3 call follows the functional; PBEsol corrects 7 of the 8
persistent CsSnBr3 errors and the ORB-v2 600 K newly-wrong one, introduces no error anywhere, and
at 600 K (308 K above the 292 K transition, label not in doubt) all four PBE CsSnBr3 errors go. So
the CsSnBr3 errors cannot be assigned to the screen. The MLIPs reproduce the PBE tilt well (three
within 20 % at the sampled amplitudes), consistent with an error they share with PBE.
Cannot claim: that PBEsol is right or that the MLIPs are "wrong because PBE is wrong". The PBEsol
numbers use PBE-generated pseudopotentials and each MLIP's lattice, which is larger than PBE's and
presumably larger still than PBEsol's own (not computed); the lattice and the functional are
entangled for PBEsol in a way they are not for PBE. One semilocal pair, no hybrid, no experiment on
the well depth.

### 2.3 PBE lattice (pl_, 3 jobs; phase A only)

PBE cubic a: BaTiO3 4.0237, KNbO3 4.0269, CsSnBr3 5.8871 A (pressure +-0.02 kbar). MLIP lattices
used by C3a are larger by 0.26-0.31 % (BaTiO3), 0.74-0.80 % (KNbO3), 0.12-0.57 % (CsSnBr3, CHGNet
the largest). Phases B (PBE force constants) and C (E(Q) along the PBE eigenvector at the PBE
lattice) were never run, so the lattice/eigenvector question is open. Plausible but unmeasured:
ferroelectric wells deepen with volume, so the smaller PBE lattice would make the KNbO3 600 K
wells shallower and that "stable" call harder to reverse; do not put this in the paper unmeasured.

### 2.4 What E2 does to "CsSnBr3 errors persist on PBE" and to the CsSnBr3 label

- "Persist on PBE": still true and numerically robust (calls unchanged under k and ecut). The
  attached attribution "belongs to the screen, the functional or the label" should be narrowed for
  CsSnBr3 to "follows the functional; not shown to be the screen's". KNbO3 600 K becomes the one
  persistent case that survives convergence and both functionals (4/5 models): the cleanest
  candidate for a screen (single-mode) error, or a label issue, though the 708 K label is not
  marginal at 600 K.
- CsSnBr3 label: unchanged (stable iff T >= 292 K). E2 removes the label as an explanation at 600 K
  (PBE wrong, PBEsol right, label 308 K from the transition). At 300 K the 8 K margin still applies;
  PBEsol agrees with the label there for 4/5 models, ORB-v2 stays unstable under both. Do not
  re-label anything.
- H2 bookkeeping (17 units at 300 K): 8 vanish on PBE; of the 5 CsSnBr3, 4 are correct on PBEsol
  and 1 (ORB-v2) persists under both; the other 4 lie outside the PBE systems.
