Notes.

- Baseline reproduction: 400/400 unit calls and 1512/1512 mode-T condensation calls match the ledger.
- (a) The kept point set is identical at 3x, 5x and 8x (60 meV floor) for 261/378 modes, including all 101 modes shallower than 7.5 meV; the multiplier sweep cannot move them.
- (b) Wells still descending at the largest sampled Q (0.45 A): 77/378 modes. Q <= 0.30 and Q <= 0.35 A keep the same 8 points and are identical by construction.
- (c) Rescaling the normalisation cell by n maps (a, b, c, M) to n(a, b, c, M), which is the minimal-cell problem at mass n^2 M and temperature T/n; checked on the SrTiO3/MACE-MP-0 R mode (solver identity passes; MACE-MP-0 energy and mass ratios for a doubled cell 2.000000 and 2.000000).
- (d) Every non-zero centroid is >= 0.035 A, so thresholds of 0.5-3 steps (0.0025-0.015 A) cannot change a call. Unit order parameters at the 0.6 A box edge: 26/400; beyond the sampled 0.45 A: 60/400; widening the box to 1.2 A moves none of them off condensation. brentq falls back to the grid-nearest width on 8441/182952 centroid evaluations (4.6%), at the deciding argmin on 0/1512 (production convention; 8/1512 under the common-FC convention).
- Unit flips are repeated measurements on few systems (all T): sampled Q <= 0.25 A: 8 over 4 (cspbi3_cubic, cssnbr3_cubic, cssni3_cubic, ti_bcc); sampled Q <= 0.30 A: 2 over 1 (cssnbr3_cubic); sampled Q <= 0.35 A: 2 over 1 (cssnbr3_cubic); sampled Q <= 0.40 A: 1 over 1 (cssnbr3_cubic); common FC supercell (q-search cell): 41 over 5 (batio3_cubic, hfo2_cubic, knbo3_cubic, pbtio3_cubic, ti_bcc); per formula unit: 89 over 12 (batio3_cubic, cspbi3_cubic, cssnbr3_cubic, cssni3_cubic, hf_bcc, hfo2_cubic, knbo3_cubic, pbtio3_cubic, srtio3_cubic, ti_bcc, zr_bcc, zro2_cubic); doubled minimal cell: 23 over 8 (batio3_cubic, cssnbr3_cubic, cssni3_cubic, hfo2_cubic, knbo3_cubic, pbtio3_cubic, srtio3_cubic, ti_bcc); 8x minimal cell (supplementary): 58 over 8 (batio3_cubic, cssnbr3_cubic, cssni3_cubic, hfo2_cubic, knbo3_cubic, pbtio3_cubic, srtio3_cubic, ti_bcc).
- The T <= 300 K accuracy column is nearly blind to over-condensation: only 9/70 of its truly stable units carry a screened imaginary mode (cssnbr3_cubic, srtio3_cubic at 300 K); the rest have no imaginary commensurate mode and cannot condense under any convention. Intervals are unit-level Wilson and do not account for clustering by system. Over the full ladder the extra condensation of larger normalisation cells shows up as false-unstables at 600-900 K (production 28/190, common FC 41/190, doubled 36/190, 8x 60/190), and all-T accuracy is 243/300, 252/300, 249/300 and 234/300 respectively. How accuracy ranks the conventions depends on which temperatures are scored, so it is not a test of which normalisation is physically right.
